— Notes on the gallery

A working portfolio of school-website ideas.

Every mockup here is a different answer to the same question: how could a school feel on the web, beyond the brochure?

The first eleven explore directory-scale interactions — for browsing many schools at once. The final six are built for a single school, Gymnasium & Internat Kloster Disentis, drawing on their own content as a worked example.

All are interactive. Many are scroll-choreographed. None require a click to make the page come alive.
