§ I · NW Quadrant
— LIVING · Upper & Lower Houses

Single rooms, private bathrooms.

Our boarders stay in comfortable single rooms equipped with their own private bathroom. The award-winning Upper and Lower Houses offer modern and thoughtfully designed living spaces. Students also have access to quiet areas for relaxation, leisure facilities, and welcoming common rooms for socialising.

  • — RoomsSingle · private bath
  • — HousesUpper & Lower · award-winning
  • — HealthIn-house monastery infirmary
  • — StaffTrained nursing professionals

§ II · NE Quadrant
— LEARNING · The Swiss Matura

Step by step, toward university.

Our primary goal: to guide students through their Gymnasium years to successfully earn the Swiss Matura, granting them direct access to university. The prefect team supervises the study halls, providing structure and guidance.

  • — GoalSwiss Matura · university access
  • — Study1.5 – 2.5 hours per day
  • — SupportPrefect team · mentoring
  • — MethodTailored to individual learners

§ III · SE Quadrant
— DOING · The Surselva, outdoors

Climbing wall to Ski Arena.

The Surselva is one of Switzerland's most attractive leisure regions and a true insider tip for outdoor sports enthusiasts. On campus: gymnasium with climbing wall, billiard, table tennis, fitness room, and a grass football pitch with breathtaking mountain panorama.

  • — WinterSki Arena · Sedrun
  • — On campusClimbing wall · fitness · grass pitch
  • — SummerMTB · golf · outdoor climbing
  • — WeatherSun instead of fog
No. 03

§ IV · SW Quadrant
— BELONGING · Klausabend & the year

Hut evenings, Klausabend, two festivals.

In autumn, "hut evenings" are a cherished tradition — far from everyday stress, focused on warmth and togetherness. Before Christmas, our Klausabend is a must. In the warmer months, football tournaments take place on the natural grass field, and our two annual school festivals bring the entire community together on the historic Lindenplatz.

  • — AutumnHut evenings
  • — Pre-ChristmasKlausabend
  • — SummerFootball tournaments · Lindenplatz
  • — Weekends2 – 3 community trips per year
No. 04
LindenplatzHistoric plaza · school festivals
