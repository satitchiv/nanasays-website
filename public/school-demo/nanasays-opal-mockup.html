Wycombe Abbey · Reading mode
We're considering this school for our daughter who's autistic and bright. Is the pastoral set-up actually equipped for her, or is that brochure language?
Honest read: partly equipped. ISI 2024 rated safeguarding excellent and named a SENCO with three specialist staff — strong on paper. ISI · p.14
But two things to weigh: cohort size is large (650+) and boarding rhythm is intense — Saturday school + Sunday chapel. For some autistic pupils that's grounding; for others it's overwhelming. Policy · p.6
Want me to compare against Benenden or Tudor Hall, where the cohort is smaller?
