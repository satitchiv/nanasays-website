§ 01 · The Day

Seven moments, seven photos.

07:00 → 22:00 · 15 hours
07:00
— Breakfast
First light. Bell from the monastery church.

First light, warm bread.

Breakfast is served in the monastery refectory at seven sharp. Boarders gather from the Upper and Lower Houses; the bell from the monastery church marks the hour. Today's weather: sun instead of fog, as we say in Disentis.

"Life in our boarding school is a unique experience — a place where individuality and community go hand in hand."
07:00
07:45
— Classes begin
Crisp mountain air. First period.

The Matura, step by step.

Our primary goal: to guide students through their Gymnasium years to successfully earn the Swiss Matura, granting direct access to university. Mornings begin with structured periods supported by mentoring and the prefect team.

— Lunch
Sun at the zenith.

Capuns, or burgers.

Lunch at 11:45. Traditional local Capuns and Maluns, alongside pizza, burgers, and Chinese — everything is possible, and all are within a short walk of campus.

"Right next to our study hall is our very own cinema, complete with original cinema seats."

13:10
— Afternoon
Long shadows on the slopes.

Climbing wall, natural grass.

Classes resume at 1:10 PM, then afternoon snack at 2:35. After 4:15 PM, free time begins: gymnasium with climbing wall, billiard, table tennis, fitness room, and a grass football pitch with mountain panorama.

17:30
— Study hall
Golden hour over the monastery roofs.

1.5 to 2.5 hours, at your desk.

Supervised study at 5:30 PM in our study halls. For students in 1st to 3rd Gymnasium, we recommend 1.5 to 2.5 hours per day. Once independent study habits are established, students may work in other rooms or in their own dormitories.

17:30
18:30
— Dinner
Sky deepens. The valley quiets.

Together at the long tables.

Dinner at 6:30 PM, then a final study period from 7:30 to 8:30. After that: free time, late snack — and on autumn evenings, the cherished tradition of hut evenings in the surrounding mountains.

— Lights out
Stars over the monastery.

Single rooms, private bathrooms.

Lights out at 10:00 PM. Each boarder has a single room with their own bathroom in the award-winning Upper and Lower Houses. The monastery's in-house infirmary — staffed by trained nursing professionals — is available day and night.

"Comfortable single rooms equipped with their own private bathroom — modern and thoughtfully designed living spaces."
