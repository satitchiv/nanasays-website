— The reading
From the ISI report, the fee schedule, and the boarding handbook.

What the brochure leaves out.

Saturday school is mandatory at Wycombe Abbey — and that includes weekly boarders. The brochure phrases it as "a full and varied week"; the boarding handbook lists 8.30 to 12.20 on the Saturday timetable.

The bursary cap reaches 100%. The pastoral lead is named in the 2024 ISI report. The library is open seven days a week. These are the kinds of things that decide a fit — not the architecture in the prospectus photographs.
